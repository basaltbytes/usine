A reload applies `workers` changes without touching running Attempts. A Worker that keeps its name, kind, and settings with a higher `max_concurrent_attempts` is preflighted with the new count and every rostered brain, and its new slots take waiting Runs at once; with a lower count it takes a new Attempt only while it runs fewer Attempts than the new count, and each extra slot retires when its Attempt ends. A new Worker of the executor's kind is preflighted with every rostered brain and takes Attempts once it passes; the SSH composition adds back only a Worker it started with. A removed Worker takes no new Attempt and leaves the scheduler when its last Attempt ends. A changed kind or other setting, a renamed Worker, and a new Worker the executor cannot run still refuse the reload with `RestartRequiredError`, which adds `fields`, the changed paths such as `workers.build-1.destination`, and names them in its message. `ReloadPreflightError` adds `workers`, the Workers the reload adds or gives more slots, and its `agents` can be empty. `InstanceReload` adds `workers`. `CurrentInstance.registerPreflight` is replaced by `registerWorkers`, which takes a `WorkerReload`, and `stepExecutorLayer` takes `StepExecutorOptions` with `startupWorkersOnly`.
