`StepExecutor.reserve` takes a second argument, `worker: Option<WorkerName>`. With a Worker, the lease comes from that Worker only, and the reservation waits while none of its slots is free. The orchestrator's scheduler serves a reservation that names a Worker before the reservations that name none, and a reservation that waits for a full Worker does not hold up the Runs behind it that another Worker can take. A reservation that names a Worker `usine.yaml` does not declare logs `Run waits for a slot on the Worker its reattached Attempt runs on, which usine.yaml does not declare; it waits until a reload declares that Worker again or the Run is canceled` at warning. A canceled reservation leaves the scheduler's queue. `StepExecutor` also gains `occupy(worker)`, which takes a slot of a Worker at once for an Attempt that already runs there, even when none is free. The scheduler then counts that Attempt, so the Worker takes no new Attempt until enough of its Attempts end, and logs `An Attempt that already runs on the Worker occupies a slot beyond its max_concurrent_attempts; the Worker takes no new Attempt until enough of its Attempts end` at warning when the slot goes beyond them. For a Worker it does not have, it occupies no slot and logs a warning.
