Configuration checks, worker preflight, and coordinator startup admit brains through role declarations. A refusal names the missing capability, placement, or credential route with the harness's reason. For example, a Pi child placed on an SSH or container worker is refused with "Pi runs its tools in the orchestrator process and supports only local children".
